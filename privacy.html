Privacy policy

Multimeedia uploader · Mammal Manual · Effective 25 October 2026

Multimeedia uploader ("the app") is a private tool used by the owner of the Mammal Manual YouTube channel to upload and manage the channel's own videos. It has no other users. This policy explains what the app accesses and how.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google and grants access, the app uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to:

  • read the owner's channel identity (to make sure uploads go to the right channel);
  • upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions, playlists and publish times;
  • read statistics for the owner's own videos (views, watch time).

The app does not access any other Google data, other users' data, comments of other people beyond what YouTube shows publicly, or any payment information.

How the data is stored

The sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. The app has no server and no database outside that computer. Video files and their texts are prepared locally by the owner.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share any data with anyone. Data goes only between the owner's computer and Google's YouTube services. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file removes the app's stored sign-in.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact
